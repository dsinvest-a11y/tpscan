How to use this report: each row is one ticker; the lines inside it are that ticker's price zones — areas where buying (demand) or selling (supply) previously concentrated. IN ZONE means the price is inside the zone right now; APPROACHING means it is closing in; WATCH means it is on the radar but further away. Score rates the zone's quality; only zones scoring 16+ (of 20) are listed, so the visible range runs 16–20 by design. Tickers are ordered by how close price is to a zone, weighted toward higher timeframes — a monthly or weekly zone nearby ranks ahead of an equally close 30-minute zone. A red ⚠ warning means an opposing zone sits close by (measured in multiples of the zone's own height), so the price may have little room to run — those entries rank lower. Tap a ticker for its full zone report with charts. Filter with the tiles, timeframe boxes and toggles; click Score or Distance to sort.
