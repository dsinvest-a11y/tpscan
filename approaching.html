How to use this report: each row is one ticker; the lines inside it are that ticker's price zones — areas where buying (demand) or selling (supply) previously concentrated. A red ⚠ warning means an opposing zone sits close by (measured in multiples of the zone's own height), so the price may have little room to run — those entries rank lower.
